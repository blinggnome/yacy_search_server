Crawler Content Rejection

Plain text rules entered here are matched case-insensitively during crawling. Soft rules reject the matching page and remove an existing indexed record for that URL. Poison pills are stronger: a match rejects the page, adds the host to url.poison_pill.black, and removes indexed records for that host. Whitelisted hosts skip poison pill host-wide cleanup, but soft rules and normal crawler cleanup still apply.
